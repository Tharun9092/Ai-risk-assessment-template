# AI Risk Assessment Tool for SMEs

## Overview

This project presents a lightweight, human-centred AI risk assessment tool designed to support small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in identifying and evaluating key risks associated with the development or use of AI systems.

The tool is designed with the requirements and risk-management principles of the European Union Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act) in mind. It aims to make AI risk assessment more accessible to SME managers and other non-technical users who may not have specialist legal or technical expertise.

## Purpose

The purpose of this artefact is to provide SMEs with a simple and practical way to:

- Identify important AI-related risks.
- Consider relevant governance and accountability measures.
- Assess human oversight and transparency.
- Consider data governance and documentation.
- Identify areas requiring further attention.
- Obtain an indicative assessment of their current AI risk-readiness.
- Generate a summary report of the assessment.

## Assessment Structure

The assessment contains 10 questions organised into key risk areas:

1. AI System Classification
2. Governance and Accountability
3. Human Oversight
4. Data Governance
5. Data Governance
6. Transparency and Explainability
7. Transparency and Human Review
8. Documentation and Record Keeping
9. Post-Deployment Monitoring
10. Incident Management and Review

For each question, users can select:

- Yes
- Partial
- No

Users can also provide optional evidence or notes to support their response.

## Human-Centred Design

The tool follows a human-centred approach by focusing on usability, accessibility and practical decision-making.

The assessment uses:

- One question per page
- Clear categories
- Simple response options
- Explanations of why each question matters
- Progress indication
- Evidence and notes fields
- A final summary of identified areas
- Recommended actions

The intention is to make AI risk assessment understandable to users without requiring advanced technical knowledge.

## EU AI Act Alignment

The assessment questions are informed by relevant requirements and concepts within the EU AI Act, including areas such as:

- AI system classification
- Risk management
- Human oversight
- Transparency
- Data governance
- Documentation
- Monitoring
- Incident management

The tool is intended as an educational and assessment-support prototype. It does not provide legal advice, formal certification, or a definitive determination of compliance with the EU AI Act.

## Assessment Output

After completing the assessment, the tool provides an indicative readiness result together with:

- Assessment ID
- Assessment date
- Individual responses
- Evidence and notes
- Identified priority areas
- Suggested actions
- Overall assessment summary

A downloadable assessment report can also be generated.

## Technology

The prototype is implemented as a lightweight web-based application using:

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript

The tool is designed to operate through a standard web browser without requiring users to install additional software.

## Data Collection

The current version is primarily a prototype for demonstrating the assessment methodology and user interface.

Automated integration with an institutional response-storage system is planned as a future enhancement. Any future participant data collection will follow the applicable research ethics, data protection and university requirements.

## Research Context

This artefact was developed as part of an MSc Human-Centred Artificial Intelligence thesis investigating:

**Designing Lightweight AI Risk Assessment Templates for SMEs under the EU AI Act**

The research explores how existing AI risk assessment approaches can be simplified and operationalised for SME environments while maintaining alignment with trustworthy and human-centred AI principles.

## Disclaimer

This tool is a research prototype and should not be interpreted as legal advice or as a substitute for professional EU AI Act compliance assessment.

The results provide an indicative view of areas that may require further consideration and do not constitute a formal assessment of legal compliance.
